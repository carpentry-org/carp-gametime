Clock

is a high-precision game timer for tracking delta-time, total time, and FPS.

Precision & Stability

The Clock stores time internally as raw Uint64 nanoseconds to avoid the cumulative drift associated with floating-point summation. It also provides delta clamping to prevent physics instability during frame spikes (e.g., when the debugger is paused).

Dual-Time Tracking

The module distinguishes between two types of total time:

  1. Simulation Total (total): Accumulates clamped delta-times. This represents the "logical" time the simulation has experienced.
  2. Real Total (real-total): Accumulates actual wall-clock time. Useful for profiling, telemetry, and non-simulated UI.

Triple-Delta Semantics

The module provides three distinct views of the time elapsed since the last tick:

  1. Raw DT (raw-dt): Actual wall-clock seconds (unclamped, unscaled).
  2. Clamped DT (clamped-dt): Simulation-safe seconds (clamped by max-dt, unscaled).
  3. Scaled DT (scaled-dt): Final gameplay seconds (clamped AND multiplied by scale).

Reset & Totals

reset! skips large time gaps by updating last-ns. Note that reset! does NOT reset the total or real-total accumulators, as they represent the lifetime of the clock, not the current frame gap.
