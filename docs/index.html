carp-gametime

Frame timing for Carp: delta time, time scaling and FPS.

(load "git@github.com:carpentry-org/carp-gametime@master")

Clock is the whole library. It keeps time internally as raw Uint64 nanoseconds, so a long session does not accumulate the drift that summing Double seconds would, and it clamps the delta so a frame spike (a breakpoint, a stalled load) cannot blow up a physics step.

Three deltas come out of one tick, and they are not interchangeable: raw-dt is the wall clock, clamped-dt is what the simulation should trust, and scaled-dt is clamped-dt times the time scale, which is what gameplay should use.

(let-do [c (Clock.new)]
  (while true
    (do
      (Clock.tick! &c)
      (update-world (Clock.scaled-dt &c))
      (draw (Clock.smooth-fps &c)))))
